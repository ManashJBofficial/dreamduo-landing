LONG-DISTANCE CARE PACKAGE PACKING CHECKLIST
DreamDuo: https://dreamduo.app/blog/long-distance-care-package-ideas

CHOOSE THE CONTENTS
[ ] A familiar item: a hoodie, scarf or something personal.
[ ] A favourite treat or sealed snacks from your area.
[ ] A keepsake, such as a framed photo or personalised keyring.
[ ] One short note about why you chose the gifts.

PACK THE BOX
[ ] Check preferences, clothing sizes, ingredients and allergies.
[ ] Keep scented fabric separate from food.
[ ] Leave snacks sealed and check their expiry dates.
[ ] Cushion fragile items and prevent movement inside the parcel.
[ ] Confirm the address and delivery arrangements.
[ ] Check carrier and destination rules for each item.
[ ] Complete any required customs paperwork accurately.
[ ] Keep the parcel tracking reference.
