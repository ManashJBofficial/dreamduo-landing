Your 30-day question calendar
DreamDuo · dreamduo.app/blog
One question a day
 Both answer. Ask a follow-up. Skip or pause whenever you like.
 Days 1–6: Light conversations
 What's something you could talk about forever without getting bored?
 What's a job you'd secretly love to try for a day?
 What's a food you think is way overrated?
 Which season feels the most like you?
 What's something you're weirdly picky about?
 Where could you spend hours without getting bored?
 Days 7–12: Funny what-ifs
 If animals could talk, which one would be the rudest?
 100 duck-sized horses, or one horse-sized duck, which fight are you taking?
 If you could rename yourself anything, what would you pick?
 If you had to come back as a vegetable, which one would you be?
 What would you want to be ridiculously famous for?
 If you had to live inside a movie's world, which one?
 Days 13–18: Emotional closeness
 What's one small thing I do that you secretly love?
 What's something I do without realizing that always makes you smile?
 When do you feel most loved by me?
 What kind of attention from me means the most to you?
 When you're having a rough day, what from me helps the most?
 How can you tell when I'm really listening to you?

 Memories and future plans
 Days 19–24: Memories
 What do you remember most clearly from the day we met?
 What was your life like around the time we met?
 What did you notice about me before we even spoke?
 Was there anything funny about how we first crossed paths?
 What was your real first impression of me?
 What small early memory of us still makes you smile?
 Days 25–30: Future plans
 Which kind of day do you wish you had more often?
 Which part of your future are you most curious about?
 At 60, what would you hope your ordinary Tuesday looks like?
 Which city would you want to try living in for a year?
 What's one thing your dream home absolutely has to have?
 What would you want our home to feel like when we walk through the door?
