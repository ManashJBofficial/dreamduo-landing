Make the next visit a plan
 Dates, number of nights, who travels and where we stay

 Four weeks out · Make the decisions
 ☐ Agree on the dates, number of nights and who travels.
 ☐ Confirm leave and accommodation availability.
 ☐ Set a total spending limit and a contribution each person agrees to.
 ☐ Check what is refundable before committing money.
 ☐ Give the next booking task to one person, with a date to report back.
 Three weeks out · Secure the essentials
 ☐ Book the agreed travel and accommodation when you're both ready.
 ☐ Save confirmations somewhere both partners can access.
 ☐ Agree on arrival and departure transport, including a backup.
 ☐ Note payment deadlines and cancellation terms in your own words.
 ☐ Keep spending money separate from money needed for the journey home.
 Two weeks out · Plan a little, leave room
 ☐ Each choose one thing you'd most like to do.
 ☐ Reserve only activities that actually need booking.
 ☐ Agree on any work or family time during the visit.
 ☐ Leave an arrival buffer and at least one open block of time.
 ☐ Check whether you need to bring anything for staying at your partner's home.
 The final week · Reduce last-minute decisions
 ☐ Recheck travel times and the meeting point.
 ☐ Share a short packing list and any practical needs.
 ☐ Decide on a simple first meal rather than a packed first evening.
 ☐ Save essential booking details offline.
 ☐ Agree on the first call after returning home, if you'd both like one.
 Start earlier when your route or requirements need it. Confirm current official travel requirements before booking.
