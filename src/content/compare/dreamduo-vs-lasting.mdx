These apps serve different needs: Lasting offers structured relationship learning, while DreamDuo helps you organise shared plans and daily routines.

Lasting can suit couples who want to explore communication and connection, whether they are facing a difficulty or simply want to learn. DreamDuo centres on practical goals such as saving for a home, planning a trip or building a habit.

Neither a goal tracker nor a self-guided course replaces individual advice from a qualified professional. Choose based on the support you actually want, rather than assuming one app covers every need.

## What Lasting does well

Lasting offers self-guided sessions for couples. Its published materials describe foundations in attachment theory and emotionally focused therapy, with research from several sources. Calling it only “the Gottman method” would miss that wider basis.

The lessons cover communication, conflict, trust, money, family and emotional connection. Its App Store description lists five free Foundations lessons, conversation prompts, reminders and standalone sessions. Check the content currently available in your account.

**Lasting also advertises live therapist-led workshops**, including opportunities for questions. That differs from ongoing one-to-one care with an assigned therapist. Check the current workshop schedule and what your subscription includes.

## Where DreamDuo is different

DreamDuo doesn't try to diagnose or fix anything. It's about day-to-day life together.

The main part is shared goals. You set one up, you both add progress, and the app shows whether you're on track. Around that sit smaller daily habits:

- A daily question you both answer privately, then reveal together.
- Quick nudges with text, photos or sounds, plus mood tracking and polls for fast decisions.
- **Milestone messages**, which open when your partner reaches a selected milestone.

The emphasis is on routines and progress. DreamDuo does not provide a structured relationship curriculum or therapist-led workshops.

## Price

DreamDuo Pro is listed at $3.99 monthly or $39.99 annually in the US App Store. Lasting lists several in-app purchase amounts, but the listing does not consistently identify their billing periods. We cannot reliably label $11.99 as its current monthly price or $79.99 as its annual price. Check the plan, renewal period and any trial terms at checkout.

## Which one should you pick?

Pick **Lasting** if you want structured exercises and relationship education, and its current content or workshops match what you want to work on.

Pick **DreamDuo** if you want a shared place for goals, practical plans and everyday check-ins.

You can use these tools at the same time or choose just one. The useful distinction is the kind of support you want, not whether your relationship is “good” or “bad”.
