## DreamDuo: for couples working toward something

Most couples apps focus on the relationship itself. DreamDuo focuses on the plans you share: a house deposit, a trip, a new habit, a move.

![The Dreams screen in DreamDuo, showing goal templates, collections, and two shared goals called Complete Bucket List Items and Joint Safety Net](dreamduo-dreams-screen.webp "The Dreams screen, where your shared goals live")

You set up a goal (the app calls it a "dream") and you both add your progress to it. There are four kinds of goal. That matters more than it sounds, because saving money and sticking to a habit go wrong in different ways.

- **Amount**, for anything with a number, like a house deposit.
- **Checklist**, for projects with steps, like a wedding or a move.
- **Habit**, for things you want to keep doing, like walking four evenings a week.
- **Time**, for anything you measure in hours.

Two features make milestones more personal. First, you can add to a goal in secret. Your partner won't see what you've put in until you decide to show them, so you can save up for something and surprise them.

Second, you can write a message and lock it to a milestone: 25, 50, 75 or 100 percent. When your partner hits that point, the message opens, even if you wrote it weeks ago.

There's also a daily question. You both answer privately, then you see each other's answers. Paired does this too, and does it in much more depth.

**Price.** The free plan gives you 3 active goals, 3 collections, 100MB of storage each and 5 hidden messages. Pro is $3.99 a month or $39.99 a year. It adds unlimited goals and collections, 2GB of storage each, more detailed stats and extra sounds. It's on iOS and Android.

**Downsides.** It's a new app. Paired has a huge user base and years of content behind it, and we don't have either yet. If you want guided relationship lessons written by therapists, DreamDuo isn't built for that, and Lasting or Paired will suit you much better. It also doesn't link to your bank, so you enter your savings yourself, while Honeydue can import bank balances and transactions from supported accounts. That is not the same as tracking a named savings goal.

**Good for** couples who keep setting goals and forgetting about them a few weeks later.

## Paired: for talking more

[Paired](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/paired-couples-relationship/id1469609343) focuses on daily conversations and relationship content. Its [feature guide](https://support.paired.com/en/articles/164632-what-is-paired) also describes a timeline, special dates and Premium relationship insights.

The idea is simple. You get one question a day, you both answer it, and you can't see your partner's answer until you've given yours. That way neither of you gets swayed by the other. The questions are written with help from relationship therapists.

The paid version adds guided courses on topics like money and love languages, plus quizzes and games.

**Price.** Paired's [US App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/paired-couples-relationship/id1469609343) lists Premium Monthly at $14.99 and multiple annual offers. Check the offer and renewal price shown to your account.

**Trade-off.** Paired focuses on conversations and relationship learning. It tracks dates and relationship insights, but we did not find a shared savings, checklist or habit-goal system in its published feature guide. Choose it for its conversation content, rather than expecting a practical project tracker.

**Good for** couples who want a small daily habit that gets them talking about things they'd usually skip.

## Honeydue: for money

[Honeydue](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/honeydue-couples-finance/id1157633945) is the bank-connected option in this selection. Its [website](https://www.honeydue.com/how-it-works) advertises more than 20,000 financial institutions across five countries: the US, Canada, UK, Spain and France. Check support for your own bank; we did not test connections.

You can both see your accounts in one place, shared and personal: balances, spending, credit cards, loans and investments. Each of you chooses what the other can see, so you don't have to share everything.

What makes it work day to day is that you can comment on a single charge. Instead of taking a screenshot and texting it (which can easily come across as an accusation), you just leave a note or an emoji on it. It also reminds you about bills, warns you when you get close to a spending limit you've set, and helps you split costs.

**Price.** Free, with optional recurring tips of $1 to $10 described in [Honeydue's help centre](https://support.honeydue.com/en/articles/3179714-how-does-honeydue-earn-money). That support article is older; we did not verify the current advertising model.

**Trade-off.** Its documented focus is balances, spending and bills. We did not find a dedicated savings-goal tracker in the product guide. Bank availability and connection reliability are practical things to check before relying on any account-linked app.

**Good for** couples who keep arguing about money, or who've combined their finances and lost track of where it's all going.

## Lasting: for working on the relationship

[Lasting](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/lasting-marriage-couples/id1225049619) offers structured relationship sessions. Its [help page](https://getlasting.com/help) describes foundations in attachment theory and emotionally focused therapy; its [research overview](https://getlasting.com/research) draws on several sources.

The self-guided sessions are only part of the offering. Lasting also advertises [live therapist-led workshops](https://getlasting.com/lasting-workshops-individuals-couples). Group workshops differ from ongoing one-to-one care with an assigned therapist. Check the current schedule and plan inclusions.

The lessons cover communication, conflict, making up after a fight, emotional and sexual connection, trust, money, family, and showing appreciation.

**Price.** Lasting's [US App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/lasting-marriage-couples/id1225049619) describes free Foundations sessions and paid monthly or annual subscriptions. Its purchase list shows several prices without consistently labeling billing periods. Confirm the plan, trial eligibility and renewal cost at checkout.

**Trade-off.** Structured sessions take time to work through. Lasting's published offering centres on relationship learning; we did not find shared savings or project checklists. Neither a course nor a goal-tracking app replaces personalised professional care.

**Good for** couples who want structured relationship exercises and learning, whether they are addressing a difficulty or building everyday communication.

## Between: for a private space

[Between](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=kr.co.vcnc.android.couple) has been around for more than ten years. It's the simplest idea on this list: a space only the two of you can see.

You get a private chat with voice messages and stickers, a shared photo album you can sort by date and place, a countdown for anniversaries and dates, and a shared calendar.

**Price.** [Between's US App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/between-couples-love-tracker/id458035189) includes $2.99 monthly. Its description quotes $13.99 annually, but the purchase list includes $24.99 annually. We cannot tell which offer you will receive; use the in-app checkout price. Paid features advertised include themes, longer media and ad removal.

**Trade-off.** The main features are private messaging, photos, dates and a calendar. If you already have a chat app and shared album you both like, it may duplicate them. We did not find a dedicated shared-goal tracker in the listing.

**Good for** couples who want their photos and messages somewhere other than a camera roll and a chat thread full of work messages.

## How to choose

Start with whatever has been going wrong lately.

- You keep setting goals and forgetting them: **DreamDuo**
- You've stopped talking about anything except chores and plans: **Paired**
- You want a clearer view of shared spending: **Honeydue**
- You want structured relationship exercises: **Lasting**
- You just want a private corner for the two of you: **Between**

## Couples app questions

### What is the best free couples app?

It depends on the task. Honeydue advertises free shared money tracking; DreamDuo has a free plan for shared goals; Between offers private messaging and photos with optional paid features. Check each app's current limits before choosing.

### Is DreamDuo a budgeting or banking app?

DreamDuo tracks progress toward goals, including savings targets entered by the couple. It does not connect to bank accounts or move money. Honeydue is the bank-connected option in this comparison.

### Which couples app is useful for long-distance partners?

Choose Paired for conversation prompts, Between for a private chat and photo space, or DreamDuo for planning visits and shared goals. First agree which problem you both want the app to solve.
