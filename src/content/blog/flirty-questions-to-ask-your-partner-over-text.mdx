Pick a question that sounds like something you'd actually text. If you already have their hoodie, send number two with a photo of the evidence.

1. If I knocked on your door right now, what would you do first?
2. Be honest. Am I borrowing your hoodie, or have you accepted it's mine now?
3. What do I do that makes you smile even when you're trying to be annoyed with me?
4. We've got one free hour together tomorrow. How are we spending it?
5. What were you trying to play cool about on our first date?
6. Did anything random make you think of me today?
7. Can I kiss you the second I see you, or do I have to say hi first?
8. Where's somewhere you've been that you wish I'd seen with you?
9. What would you write on a note and slip into my coat pocket?
10. Would you let your arm go numb if I fell asleep on your shoulder?
11. Which outfit of mine would you like to see again?
12. You can steal one bite off my plate. What are you hoping I order?
13. Send me a song that sounds like us. Which one comes to mind?
14. Do you like a good morning text, or a random one that catches you off guard?
15. What's the first thing you tell your friends when they ask about me?
16. Are you a hand-holder when we're out, or a pinkies-only kind of person?
17. Which bit of our first kiss do you remember best?
18. If we were sitting next to each other at a very boring dinner, what would you whisper to make me laugh?
19. You've had a long day. Do you want me to bring dinner, put the kettle on, or just come sit with you?
20. Which of us is worse at pretending we only came over to watch the movie?
21. Is there a compliment from me that stuck with you?
22. Can I take you out for breakfast in whatever we slept in?
23. What did you think my type was before you knew it was you?
24. When I'm flirting with you, what gives me away first?
25. Which ordinary day of ours would you happily do all over again?

26. How many minutes does “stay five more minutes” mean when I say it?
27. What's a phrase I say so often you can hear it in my voice?
28. What could we talk about until two in the morning without noticing the time?
29. What were you going to text me after our first date before you settled on what you sent?
30. Want a voice note or a photo of whatever I'm doing right now?
31. What tiny habit of yours have I accidentally picked up?
32. If I caught you looking at me from across the room, would you wave or pretend you were looking at something else?
33. If we swapped phones for the day, what would you change my wallpaper to?
34. Which of our inside jokes would be impossible to explain to anyone else?
35. Is there a text from me you've reread when you missed me?
36. If you had to draw me from memory, what would you get right?
37. Do you want me to plan the whole date or just tell you where to meet me?
38. What part of my day would you come along for if you could?
39. If I fall asleep texting you, what goodnight message am I waking up to?
40. Which photo of me do you like more than I do?
41. Last two people in the café. One slice of cake left. Are we sharing?
42. Was there a moment you almost asked me out and changed your mind?
43. When we hug, which one of us usually lets go first?
44. Picture us on a Sunday a few years from now. What are we doing?
45. What did you first save my name as on your phone?
46. When did you realize you could be properly silly around me?
47. If I were there tonight, could I talk you into a walk with no destination?
48. What do you like hearing me say in person more than over text?
49. If I posted you a postcard today, what would you want me to write on it?
50. What's the first thing you're looking forward to doing when we're together again?

When they answer, tell them what you were thinking too. If they say they'd let you keep the hoodie, you can always ask whether they're willing to give up the matching sweatpants.
